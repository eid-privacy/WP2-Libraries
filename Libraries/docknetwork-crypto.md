**Repository**: https://github.com/docknetwork/crypto
**Language:** Rust
**License**: Apache-2.0

**Features:**

- Sigma protocols for discrete log proofs
- BBS and BBS+ signatures for anonymous credentials
- Dynamic accumulators (positive and universal)
- Composite proof system combining primitives
- Verifiable encryption using SAVER
- Secret sharing schemes and DKG
- Coconut and PS signatures
- LegoGroth16 zkSNARK proof system
- Oblivious Transfer (OT) and Extensions (OTE)
- Short group signatures (BB and weak-BB)
- Keyed-Verification Anonymous Credentials (KVAC)
- SyRA sybil resilient signatures
- Verifiable encryption from MPC-in-the-Head

**Additional Information:**

- Supports std, no_std, and WASM builds
- Contains Circom circuits (30.6% of codebase)
- Has WASM wrapper and TypeScript bindings
