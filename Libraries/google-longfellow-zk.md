**Summary**
This library is based on the paper by Google [Anonymous credentials from ECDSA](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/2010).  
It also has a draft standard: [libZK: a zero-knowledge proof library](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-google-cfrg-libzk/).  

**Resources:**  
- Review article by [dyne.org](https://news.dyne.org/longfellow-zero-knowledge-google-zk/)  
- Book/Survey on ZKP: [Proofs, Arguments, and Zero-Knowledge](https://people.cs.georgetown.edu/jthaler/ProofsArgsAndZK.pdf)  
- Recent MIT course on ZKP and proofs in general: [Advanced Topics In Cryptography](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-5630-advanced-topics-in-cryptography-fall-2023/)  
- Recent MIT course on Elliptic Curves: [Elliptic Curves](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-783-elliptic-curves-spring-2021/)
